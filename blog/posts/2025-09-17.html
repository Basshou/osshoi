My Top 3 Favourite Ayano Moments from CUTIE STREET MVs

Context

why top 3 and not 5? because CUTIE STREET only have 6 MVs where one feels more biased towards Paru & Emiru whereas the other 2 feel more like a joint group effort, but anyways

3) We can't stop suddenly! (キューにストップできません！)

Ayano slowly tilting her head while going "Kyuu ni stop dekimasen?" after the end of the daruma-san part is such a killer, thats kinda the only notable Ayano part that I like from this, but it's so good that I'm rather fine with only having this.


2) Can You Find Our Cuteness? (かわいいさがしてくれますか?)

The Ayano peak in this happens to be bit of a wordplay(?) during the funny:

>"Kawaii sagashite kuremasu da?"

>"Ayano!"

The haha big funny joke here is that Ayano's family name is Masuda, so (I think) that the song is basically saying something like:

>"Can you look for something cute?"

>"Ayano!"

Which is kinda cheesy, but hey it's cool + having Ayano full on smilling afterwards is bless too


1) The project of the earth-makeup (ちきゅーめいくあっぷ計画)

Probably my overall favourite CUTIE STREET song, but thats beside the point. The Ayano highlight in this would be during the "daisuki ya de" followed by Ayano spam which just made me went "HOLY!"

The "Kyatto sutoriito" nearing the end of it all is pretty nice too, especially during their live performance since she speedran the expression changes and later have her hand curled up like a car too 😭


Conclusion

Ayano doesn't really have the best vocals nor does she have the best appearance, but damn she must have the best stage presence out of all CUTIE STREET girls and in my opinion, that is more than enough to solidify her as good contender for Best CUTIE STREET Girl, though despite all of that if you'd to ask me who have the 2nd best stage appearance alongside having best vocals and overall best appearance to boot then it'd...
